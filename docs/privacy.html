Privacy Policy

Effective: 27 July 2026

Beyond The Rules Uploader is a self-operated tool used solely by the owner of the YouTube channel 规则之外. It is not offered to outside users.

Google data accessed

The application requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload scope. It uses that permission to upload original videos and thumbnails to the owner's YouTube channel. It does not request or access Google profile data, contacts, email, comments, subscriptions, or analytics.

Storage and protection

The OAuth client identifier, client secret, and refresh token are stored in GitHub encrypted secrets. They are made available only to the production job at runtime and are never written into public artifacts or application logs. Upload receipts contain only the resulting video identifier and processing status.

Sharing, sale, and advertising

Google user data is not sold, rented, shared with data brokers, used for advertising, or transferred to unrelated third parties. Infrastructure providers process encrypted secrets and job data only to operate the workflow.

Retention and deletion

Generated video artifacts and technical receipts may be retained for up to 30 days for quality review. The channel owner can revoke application access at Google Account permissions. A deletion request can also be opened in the project's public issue tracker; credentials will then be revoked and deleted from the repository secrets.

Google policy

The application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
